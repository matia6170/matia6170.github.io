About Me

Hi, my name is Matia Choi. I’m studying Computer Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. I am interested in embedded systems and robotics. My fascination with embedded systems and robotics stems from a passion for transforming innovative ideas into tangible solutions that can improve everyday life. I'm proficient in C/C++, python, ROS, Fusion 360, STM32, KiCAD, and more. I'm eager to explore opportunities in summer internships and research, where I can contribute my expertise and continue to grow professionally.
